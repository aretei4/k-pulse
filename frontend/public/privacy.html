Privacy Policy

Applies to the K-Pulse Field Android app and the K-Pulse web app at direco.co.in/kpulse. Effective 15 September 2026.

K-Pulse is operated by [Organisation name], [registered address] ("we", "us"). K-Pulse is a tool for campaign field teams to record voter sentiment. It is an independent tool and is not affiliated with the Election Commission of India or any government body.

Who uses K-Pulse

K-Pulse is used by field agents and administrators whose accounts are approved by a campaign administrator. It is not intended for the general public, and not for anyone under 18.

Sentiment records describe a person's political opinion, which is sensitive personal data. We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, or device identifiers, and the app contains no advertising or analytics SDKs.

How data is shared

  • Within the campaign: administrators can see all records and reports. Agents see only the voter lists their approved access covers.
  • Service providers: data is hosted on Amazon Web Services in Mumbai (ap-south-1). Sign-in codes are delivered through an SMS provider. Pages load fonts from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address.
  • We do not sell personal data or share it for advertising. We disclose data if required by law.

Security

Data travels over HTTPS. Access is checked on the server for every request, limited to the units an agent is approved for, and expires automatically (six months by default) or when an administrator revokes it.

Your rights and deleting your account

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 you can ask to access, correct or erase your personal data, and to withdraw consent. To delete your K-Pulse account, use the delete-account page, or the "Delete your account" option on the Status screen of the app. An administrator confirms the request with you on your registered mobile number, and approval erases your account together with every entry you recorded: sentiment entries, pre-election house tallies, access requests and proposed voter changes. Nothing is kept in anonymised form. We confirm and complete deletion within [N] days. You may also email [contact email] from, or quoting, your registered mobile number.

If a record about you as a voter was entered by an agent, you can make the same requests at the same address.
