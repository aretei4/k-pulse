## Full description (max 4000)

```
K-Pulse Field is the field app for campaign volunteers and agents who record voter sentiment door to door.

Request access to a booth, panchayat, block or district for your candidate. Once your campaign's administrator approves it, the voter roll for every booth it covers opens on your phone.

WHAT YOU CAN DO
• See the voter roll for the booths you are approved for, and search by name or EPIC number
• Record each voter's sentiment toward your candidate: positive, neutral or negative, with a confidence level and residence status
• Change an entry at any time
• Propose corrections to the roll (add, edit or remove a voter) for an administrator to approve
• See booth-wise bar and pie charts of the sentiment you have recorded
• Track your access requests, approvals and expiry dates

BUILT FOR CAMPAIGN TEAMS
• Access is granted per booth, panchayat, block or district, and expires automatically
• Agents see only the voter lists they are approved for
• Every entry is tied to the agent who recorded it and the candidate the access was granted for
• Sign in with your registered mobile number and a one-time code

K-Pulse Field needs an account approved by your campaign's administrator and an internet connection.

K-Pulse is an independent tool. It is not affiliated with the Election Commission of India or any government body.
```
